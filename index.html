The Poony Championship

Where Saskatchewan's best compete.

Not Just Another Golf Tournament.

The Poony Championship was created to determine who can perform when the pressure is highest.

Eight of the top golfers from the Pony Cup are invited to compete in a three-round championship played entirely under stroke play conditions.

There are no handicap adjustments. No strokes given. No excuses.

"Three rounds. Eight players. One champion."
8

Elite Players

The field consists of eight of the top golfers from the Pony Cup.

The Championship

Everything comes down to 54 holes.

3

Rounds

Players compete over three rounds of championship stroke play.

54

Holes

Every shot counts toward the final aggregate score.

1

Champion

The lowest aggregate score wins the Poony Championship.

The Purple Jacket

The ultimate prize. The symbol of supremacy. The player with the lowest aggregate score after three rounds is crowned the Poony Champion and earns the right to wear the Purple Jacket.
